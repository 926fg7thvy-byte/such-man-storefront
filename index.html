THE BRAND

A Jamaican term for another man,

“Same man. Different story.”

SUCH MAN is a Jamaican streetwear brand built on a simple premise: the code of the culture is written in how we dress, how we speak, how we carry ourselves. Six original designs. Washed black cotton. Exposed seams, distressed hardware, ink-on-cotton typography. No fast fashion. No copycat graphics. Just the uniform for the crew.
